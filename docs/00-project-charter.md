# Project Charter

## Project Name

Project Atlas (Working Title)

---

## Vision

Build an AI-native SaaS platform that transforms customer support conversations into actionable business intelligence.

Instead of simply managing support tickets, the platform helps organizations understand what customers are experiencing, why problems occur, and what actions should be prioritized.

---

## Mission

Empower support, product, and engineering teams to make data-driven decisions using AI-generated insights from customer conversations.

---

## Problem Statement

Modern companies receive thousands of customer support conversations every month.

While existing support platforms excel at ticket management, they provide limited help in answering strategic questions such as:

- What problems are increasing?
- What features are customers requesting?
- Which documentation is missing?
- Which issues should Product prioritize?
- Which issues are frustrating customers the most?

Teams spend hours manually analyzing spreadsheets and exporting reports.

We want AI to perform this analysis automatically.

---

## Target Customers

Initially:

- SaaS startups
- Small and medium businesses
- Customer Support Managers
- Product Managers
- Engineering Managers

Future:

- Enterprise organizations
- B2B customer success teams
- Consulting companies

---

## Guiding Principles

- AI should explain its reasoning.
- Human review is always possible.
- Simple user experience.
- Fast performance.
- Production-quality engineering.
- Secure by design.
- Multi-tenant architecture from day one.

---

## Success Metrics

For MVP:

- Upload support tickets.
- AI generates actionable insights.
- Dashboard presents clear summaries.
- Reports can be exported.

For the future:

- Integrations with Zendesk, Freshdesk, Intercom, Jira, Slack and GitHub.
- Predictive analytics.
- AI agents.
- Workflow automation.

---

## Out of Scope (MVP)

- Live chat
- Ticket assignment
- Omnichannel messaging
- Help desk replacement

This platform complements existing support systems rather than replacing them.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Become the AI intelligence layer sitting above customer support systems, helping organizations convert customer conversations into strategic product and business decisions.
